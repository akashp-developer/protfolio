# Visual QA

## Method

The local production build was captured at 1533 × 760 CSS px with a device scale factor of 1.25, producing 1916 × 950 images for comparison with the recording. Captures covered the twelve homepage landmarks and the seven secondary routes shown. The comparisons use corresponding section landmarks, since section heights and text wrapping vary with Akash’s content. Mobile was compared with the live reference at 390 × 844. Desktop rollover and pointer feedback were also checked with ordinary motion enabled.

On this machine the captures are in `%TEMP%/codex-portfolio-visual-comparison`. The reference video and its extracted frames are outside the project.

## Functional checks

- TypeScript, ESLint, and the production build.
- Playwright coverage for routes and anchors, browser console during valid routes, Works and archive filters, skill and service disclosures, chapter tracking, timeline and process controls, keyboard navigation, and reduced motion.
- The embedded PDF reads the original supplied file. Browser download and the PDF response signature passed.
- The contact form’s client validation and simulated success and error responses passed. Direct requests exercised server validation, origin handling, body-size limits, and the honeypot. Live delivery requires the configured Resend sender and Upstash Redis credentials.

The archive and case-study routes remain navigable, with their own filters and verified project content. The recording shows their links, so the recording-based comparison covers the launch paths rather than the entire destination screens.
