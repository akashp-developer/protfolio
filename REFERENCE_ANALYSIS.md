## Design measurements

- Computed families are Geist Sans and Geist Mono. Observed preload files `Geist_Variable-s.p.2-i4fw5gbtlb7.woff2` and `GeistMono_Variable.p.0b02cdm7g7y6h.woff2`. Use the public Geist distribution rather than extracting font binaries from the site.
- Large display 152px, 600 weight, 139.84px line height, −6.84px tracking. Section display 84px, 600 weight, 82.32px line height, −3.36px tracking. Supporting headings 40px with 42px line height and −1.2px tracking. Works row heading reaches 80px.
- Body background RGB 11/11/12, foreground RGB 243/240/234, and footer RGB 18/18/20. Coral text reaches RGB 255/123/82 in the résumé labels. The recording shows deeper vermilion fills and charcoal divider lines, with warm off-white text.
- At a 1916 CSS px live viewport, the 1440px composition begins at x238 and its common 1328px text column begins at x294. Use 56px desktop inner gutters and smaller responsive gutters. Most secondary page display headings are limited to about 986px.
- Buttons are rounded pills, supported by understated outlined and quiet variants. Border rules, open space, typography, a fixed navigation pill, and small monospaced labels carry most of the page hierarchy. Dense cards and saturated gradients are uncommon.
- Images shown inside Akash’s implementation come from its own artwork. No profile photograph or screenshots of Akash’s projects were supplied. The original recording and the reference owner’s personal material are kept out of the distributed assets.
